# Customer Churn Analysis | Retention Risk & Customer Insights

## Engagement context

This analytical work was presented to paying clients. The public repository uses a publicly available telecommunications customer churn dataset, not confidential client records. It documents the analytical approach while keeping client information private.

## The business challenge

Customer churn affects recurring revenue, customer lifetime value and the cost of acquiring replacement customers. A business needs more than a single churn percentage: it needs to understand which customer characteristics and service patterns are associated with customers leaving.

This analysis explores how customer information can be examined to support retention planning and more focused customer engagement.

## Business questions

- What proportion of customers have churned in the analysed dataset?
- How does churn vary across customer and service characteristics?
- Which variables show meaningful relationships with churn?
- Can machine-learning methods help distinguish customers with different churn outcomes?
- Which customer segments may warrant closer retention attention?

## Analytical work

The repository contains notebooks covering:

- Exploratory churn analysis and visual examination of customer characteristics.
- Data preparation for machine-learning analysis.
- Investigation of relationships between explanatory variables, including multicollinearity.
- Model-oriented analysis of customer churn patterns.

The accompanying notebooks and visual outputs are the evidence of the work. Model performance and specific findings should be taken from the executed notebook outputs rather than inferred from the repository title.

## Business relevance

The analysis can help a business move from broad retention activity towards more informed investigation of customer groups and service experiences associated with churn. It can support:

- Identifying customer segments for retention review.
- Understanding how contract and service characteristics relate to churn.
- Prioritising customer experience questions for further investigation.
- Evaluating whether predictive modelling can assist retention teams.

This work does not claim that churn was reduced or revenue increased as a result. Those outcomes require implementation, measurement and comparison against an appropriate baseline.

## Data and interpretation

- The public repository uses a publicly available telecommunications churn dataset.
- It does not contain confidential client customer records.
- Relationships observed in the dataset are not proof of causation.
- A model's usefulness depends on its validation, class balance, chosen threshold, data quality and how predictions are used operationally.
- Any retention action should be evaluated for cost, customer impact and actual retention outcomes.
